Hello!

I’m a science fiction writer.

I write Chinese-language science fiction under the pen name 伏鳞, and I also write in English as Skye Loong.

My stories explore people encountering ideas larger than themselves—time, intelligence, consciousness, memory, evolution, and other forms of the unknown.
