Hello, I’m Skye.

I’m a science fiction writer.

I write stories about people encountering ideas larger than themselves—time, intelligence, consciousness, memory, evolution, and other forms of the unknown.

This is where I collect my fiction, notes, and works in progress. Some begin as scientific questions. Others begin with an image, a feeling, or a world that refuses to disappear.
